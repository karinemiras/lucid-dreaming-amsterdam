Art

I'd like to use this space to share some of the art inspired by my lucid dreams and out-of-body experiences. The music and videos were all created by me, based on melodies and harmonies that I heard during these experiences. I'm also including some childhood drawings and paintings that I made about them. The digital illustrations were created by me through an iterative creative process using GPT, with descriptions of these experiences serving as prompts, along with pictures of me and people close to me.

Reality test

In his head

Prophetic dream

I once dreamed about how I could have prevented my father's death, and this melody was playing in the background. When I woke up, I recorded myself humming it, and later turned it into this piece.

Sad dolls
